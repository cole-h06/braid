# BRAID Research

The [specification](../spec/README.md) owns BRAID's mathematical contract and records
open research questions. The [canonical fixtures](../tests/fixtures/README.md)
provide frozen conformance and development data.

- [Analysis](analysis/README.md): historical, reusable diagnostic calculations.
- `figures/`: research illustrations and reliability propagation animation.
- [Algorithm experiments](../experiments/algorithm/README.md): direct synthetic inputs.
- [Applied experiments](../experiments/applied/agent_dataset/README.md): agent and enterprise environments.
- [Reorganization record](reorganization.md): exact file inventory and validation.
